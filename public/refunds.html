Refund Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

MicroForge sells digital credit packs and optional subscriptions that provide access to API usage, batch processing, automation, and other premium functionality.

One-time credit packs

If you purchased a credit pack by mistake and the purchased credits have not been materially used, you may request a refund within 14 days of purchase. Once a substantial portion of the purchased credits has been consumed, the corresponding digital service has been delivered and the purchase may no longer be refundable except where required by applicable law.

Subscriptions

You may cancel a subscription at any time to prevent future renewal charges. Cancellation does not normally create a prorated refund for a billing period already started, except where required by applicable law or where a duplicate or erroneous charge occurred.

Duplicate, fraudulent, or incorrect charges

Duplicate charges, confirmed billing errors, and unauthorized transactions will be reviewed promptly. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method and are subject to the payment provider's processing time.

How to request a refund

Use the contact option available on MicroForge and include the transaction reference, purchase email, and a short description of the issue. Do not send card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.

Payment provider

Payments and refunds may be processed by Paddle, our merchant-of-record/payment provider, under its applicable buyer terms and statutory consumer protections.
